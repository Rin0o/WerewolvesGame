Werewolves of Miller's Hollow

A game night app for friends

An Android app for playing The Werewolves of Miller's Hollow together, each player on their own phone. It deals the roles, wakes each role at night, runs the day's vote, and keeps the wolves' chat secret, with an optional narrator mode for whoever wants to lead the game.

The app is shared privately with friends and isn't published on any app store.
