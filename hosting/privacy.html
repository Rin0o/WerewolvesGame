Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Werewolves of Miller's Hollow is an unofficial fan app for playing the social deduction game with friends. It is made by WillCode4Beer and shared privately; it is not sold or advertised. This page explains what the app stores, where, and how to remove it.

The short version

  • You play anonymously unless you choose to sign in with Google.
  • The app stores your chosen name and your games so they can be played and resumed.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • You can delete your account from the app's settings at any time.

What is stored on Google's servers

The app uses Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) to run games between phones.

  • An anonymous player ID, created automatically the first time you open the app. It isn't linked to your identity unless you sign in with Google.
  • Game rooms. Each room holds what the game needs: the players' chosen names, seats, roles, night actions, votes, chat messages and the game log. Rooms are kept after a game ends, and their chats are cleared when the room starts a new game.
  • If you sign in with Google (optional): Firebase Authentication keeps the email address, name and profile picture link of your Google account, joined to your player ID. The app also saves your chosen player name, so it comes back when you sign in on another phone. The app asks Google only for this basic profile; it never sees your password and can't read anything else in your Google account.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your chosen name and your sound setting.
  • Your notes, suspicions and private clues for each game.
  • The game you're in, so you can rejoin it after closing the app.

Android may include this in your phone's own backup to your Google account, depending on your phone's backup settings. Uninstalling the app removes it from the phone.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a room's QR code to join it. Nothing from the camera is stored or sent anywhere.

Who can see your data

  • Players in your room see your chosen name and the messages you write in the game's chats.
  • The app is built for casual games among friends: other users of the app could technically read the data of a game room, including roles. Please don't put anything private in your name or in chat messages.
  • Your Google email and your saved player name are visible only to you. They are never shown to other players.
  • Google stores and processes this data as the app's service provider.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, files, location or microphone.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: in the app, open Home → Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your saved name. You can keep playing anonymously afterwards.
  • Data on your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • Anything else, such as your name or messages in past game rooms: email werewolvesgame.suporte@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app starts storing anything new, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
